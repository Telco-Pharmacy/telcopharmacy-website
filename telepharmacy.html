Before Your Video Consultation

  • Ensure you have a reliable internet connection.
  • Use a device with a working camera and microphone.
  • Choose a quiet and well-lit environment.
  • Make sure your device has sufficient battery power.
  • Use an updated browser such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.
  • Allow your browser to access your camera and microphone when requested.
  • Have your medication information or relevant questions available for discussion with the pharmacist.
