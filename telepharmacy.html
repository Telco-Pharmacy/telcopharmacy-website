How to Prepare for Your Consultation

Preparing your information in advance can help make your consultation more useful.

  • Have a list of the medicines and supplements you currently use.
  • Know the name and strength of medicines you want to discuss, if available.
  • Prepare the main questions or concerns you want to discuss with the pharmacist.
  • Keep relevant medication packages or prescriptions available if needed.
  • Choose a quiet location with a reliable internet connection for your video consultation.
  • Use a device with a working camera and microphone if your consultation requires video.
