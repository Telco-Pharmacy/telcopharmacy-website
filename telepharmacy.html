At your scheduled time, open your Telco Pharmacy Doxy.me room link and join your pharmacist.

How to Prepare for Your Consultation

  • Find a quiet and well-lit place for your consultation.
  • Make sure your phone, tablet or computer has a working camera and microphone.
  • Use a stable internet connection.
  • Have your current medicines or prescription information available.
  • Prepare any questions you would like to discuss with the pharmacist.
  • Join the Doxy.me waiting room a few minutes before your scheduled appointment.

Patient Privacy & Digital Healthcare

Telco Pharmacy & Stores is committed to protecting patient privacy and continuously strengthening its digital healthcare security practices. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive medical information through general messaging channels. Use the designated consultation workflow when discussing clinical information with our pharmacy team.
