About Telco Pharmacy & Stores

Your trusted partner for professional pharmaceutical care and accessible health services.

Who We Are

Telco Pharmacy & Stores is a community-focused pharmacy committed to providing quality pharmaceutical care, genuine medicines and professional health services.

We combine professional pharmacy practice with convenient digital health services to make healthcare information and pharmaceutical support more accessible to our patients.

Our Mission

To improve the health and wellbeing of individuals and communities through accessible pharmaceutical care, genuine medicines, reliable health information and compassionate professional service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted global pharmacy brand, recognized for excellence in pharmaceutical care, healthcare innovation, patient education and responsible digital health services.

Our Commitment to Patients

We are committed to putting patients at the centre of our services by providing professional guidance, responsible medication support and reliable pharmaceutical information.

  • Professional pharmaceutical counselling
  • Genuine and quality medicines
  • Patient-focused healthcare services
  • Reliable drug information
  • Accessible digital health services
  • Convenient healthcare support

Digital Health & Patient Privacy

Our digital health platform is designed with patient privacy and data protection in mind.

We are developing our digital health services using secure cloud architecture principles and healthcare security best practices to support a trustworthy digital healthcare experience.

Our approach considers important security principles including confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity and non-repudiation.

Professional Pharmaceutical Care

Our services include medication counselling, prescription support, health checks, drug information, appointment requests and other pharmaceutical services designed to support informed and responsible healthcare decisions.
