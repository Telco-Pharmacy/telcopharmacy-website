About Telco Pharmacy

Professional Care. Trusted Service.

Serving our patients through professional pharmaceutical care, health education and digital healthcare services.

Who We Are

Telco Pharmacy & Stores is a community-focused pharmacy committed to providing quality pharmaceutical care, genuine medicines and professional health services.

We combine professional pharmacy practice with convenient digital health services to make pharmaceutical support, health information and patient care more accessible.

Our Mission

To improve the health and wellbeing of individuals and communities through accessible pharmaceutical care, genuine medicines, reliable health information and compassionate professional service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted global pharmacy brand, recognized for excellence in pharmaceutical care, healthcare innovation, patient education and responsible digital health services.

Our Commitment to Patients

✔ Professional Pharmaceutical Care

We provide patient-focused pharmaceutical guidance to support safe and responsible use of medicines.

✔ Genuine Medicines

We are committed to providing quality medicines from trusted sources.

✔ Patient Education

We provide reliable medication and health information to help patients make informed healthcare decisions.

✔ Digital Healthcare

We provide convenient digital health services designed to make pharmaceutical support more accessible.

Digital Health & Patient Privacy

Our digital health platform is built with patient privacy and data protection in mind.

We are developing our digital health services using secure cloud architecture principles and healthcare security best practices to support a trustworthy digital healthcare experience.

Our security approach considers important principles including Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Authenticity and Non-Repudiation.
